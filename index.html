Structural Engineering & Architectural Design

CONSTRUCT is a multidisciplinary engineering and architectural practice. We specialize in high-performance commercial skyscrapers, seismic base-isolation, geotechnical foundation design, and sustainable smart infrastructure built for generational permanence.

Core Engineering Disciplines

  • Structural Design & FEA Optimization: Tube-in-tube cores, outrigger systems, and algorithmic steel minimization.
  • Seismic Resilience: Base-isolated Lead Rubber Bearings (LRB) engineered to ASCE 7-22 and Eurocode standards.
  • Intelligent Responsive Façades: Double-glazed unitized systems with solar tracking and thermal barrier optimization.
  • Digital Twins & Sensor Networks: Real-time structural health monitoring via low-latency telemetry and ISO 19650 BIM pipelines.
